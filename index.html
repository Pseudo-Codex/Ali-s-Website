About Us

Founded in 2010 , Anoma DriveTech is one of Ethiopia's most respected company which is involved in import and export and suppliers of mainly aluminum works, shop display fittings, wood works, shelving systems, led screens, furnitures and office supplies . With decades of expe-rience, we import and distribute high-quality goods sourced from leading manufacturers across Asia serving professionals, contractors, New retail shops and institutions nationwide. What sets us apart is our unwavering commitment to quality and customer service. We listen closely to our clients' needs and consistently strive to exceed expectations. Our reputation is built on trust, reliability, and a dedication to helping Ethiopia move for-ward.
